# Cover letters

A cover letter is built **per posting** and lives in CV Studio next to your
CVs — it shares their templates, design tokens, versions and exports.

## Start a letter

There are two entry points:

- **CV Studio → New cover letter** — pick one of your **saved** postings and,
  optionally, a CV to reuse as the base context.
- **Any posting → Draft cover letter** — opens the letter builder for the
  posting you are looking at.

Either way the letter document is created immediately and the builder opens
in letter mode.

## The brief

The left pane is a deterministic grounding pack, never an AI guess:

- The posting's **must-haves** with their evidence quotes
- Your **fit** for the role, per dimension
- Your **goal** for the application

## Evidence-cited drafts

**Draft with AI** proposes a salutation, body paragraphs and a closing. Every
paragraph cites the profile items it rests on; a paragraph with no evidence
behind it is **flagged and cannot be applied**. You pick which paragraphs to
include — drafts run through the audited AI pipeline like every other AI
action, and nothing is written to the letter until you apply it.

## Edit, render, export

The **Sections** tab edits the letter block directly (recipient and closing
details, paragraphs). Letters render through the **same template engine** as
your CV, using your CV's design tokens, so a letter and a CV look like a
matched set. They share the same **versions** and **exports** (PDF, DOCX,
Markdown, JSON, ATS text).
