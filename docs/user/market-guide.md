# Market guide: UK and DACH

The **Market guide** (`/guides/market`) is a short, sourced orientation for
applying in the United Kingdom and the German-speaking DACH markets
(Germany, Austria and Switzerland). It is general guidance, **not legal or
immigration advice**, and it does not replace the advert, an employer's
instructions or an official credential assessment.

Sources were reviewed on **12 November 2026**. Every card links the page that
supports it; the grade table links the credential authorities separately.

  [Prospects: graduate schemes](https://www.prospects.ac.uk/careers-advice/getting-a-job/graduate-schemes/).

## Grade orientation

The guide shows a rough orientation between UK honours classifications and
the **German** decimal Noten scale and the **Austrian** whole-number scale:

| UK classification | German grade | Austrian grade |
|---|---|---|
| First (70–100%) | 1.0–1.5 | 1 |
| Upper second 2:1 (60–69%) | 1.6–2.5 | 2 |
| Lower second 2:2 (50–59%) | 2.6–3.5 | 3 |
| Third (40–49%) | 3.6–4.0 | 4 |

German grades run from **1.0 (best)** to **4.0 (pass)**; **5.0 is a fail**.
Austrian university grades are whole numbers from **1 (best)** to **4
(pass)**; **5 is a fail**. The table is for orientation only — it is not an
official conversion. Employers and credential services make the formal
decision; the page links [UK ENIC](https://www.enic.org.uk/) and
[anabin](https://anabin.kmk.org/anabin.html) for that.

Swiss grades run from **6.0 (best)** to **4.0 (pass)** and are not mapped
one-to-one to UK classes. Your own profile stores the formal grade as a scale
plus value; the app never fabricates a class or converts between scales for
eligibility. See [Onboarding and your profile](onboarding-and-profile.md).

## What each market card covers

- **CV conventions** — expected length and order, whether a photo is common,
  and which attachments belong in the initial application.
- **Application norms** — the usual channel (portal, form or email), whether
  a cover letter is expected, and how work authorization is handled.
- **Hiring cycle** — when graduate schemes, internships, apprenticeships and
  dual-study places typically open and start.

Choose a market with the tabs. Each card names its own source. The whole page
is localized in English, German and Greek; quoted local terms such as
*tabellarischer Lebenslauf*, *Bewerbung* and *Lehre* stay in their original
language for recognition.
