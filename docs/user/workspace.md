# The workspace

The workspace is what you land on after signing in: the dashboard at
`/` and the working surfaces it links to — the assistant chat
(`/chat`), the rankings table (`/rankings`) and the compare tray
(`/compare`). This page gives the one-screen tour; each surface's full
guide is linked below.

## The dashboard (`/`)

The dashboard is your working desk, not a landing page. What it shows
depends on where you are:

- **Career-stage greeting** — the tagline follows the career stage you
  picked during [onboarding](onboarding-and-profile.md) (student,
  early-career, experienced, switching, returning).
- **Completeness banner** — while your profile has required sections
  left, a banner counts them and links back into the
  [onboarding wizard](onboarding-and-profile.md). Every section you
  finish sharpens matching, scoring and suggestions.
- **Quick actions** — generate catalog jobs with AI
  (`/catalog/generate`), start a CV in
  [CV Studio](cv-studio.md), open the rankings and, when the
  universities feature is on for your stage, the
  [universities workspace](universities.md).
- **Top matches** — your four highest-ranked catalog jobs with their
  score bubbles, stale-fit badges and a **See all** link into
  [rankings](matching-and-rankings.md). Nothing scored yet? The empty
  state sends you to score your first jobs.
- **New for you** — the discovery feed (toggle between new matches and
  what you saved). Opening a card marks it seen and jumps to its job
  page.
- **Target mode** — with a target set via
  [express start](onboarding-and-profile.md), the dashboard switches to
  the target lens: open live postings for your target, its salary band,
  top employers, adjacent careers to expand into, your completeness
  ring and the next micro-step.
- **Suggested next steps** — the assistant's candidate suggestions, and
  the real **saved → applied → interviewing → offers** funnel from
  [your applications](applications.md).

## Chat, rankings, compare

Three surfaces sit one click from the dashboard and carry the day's
work:

- **Assistant chat** (`/chat`) — the grounded assistant: ask about your
  catalog, postings and profile; profile edits always arrive as review
  cards. See [Chat and proposals](assistant-chat.md).
- **Rankings** (`/rankings`) — every catalog job with its AI score,
  your own score and interest status, sliced by filters. See
  [Matching and rankings](matching-and-rankings.md).
- **Compare tray** (`/compare`) — pick 2–4 jobs anywhere in the catalog
  and open them side by side, per dimension. Also covered in
  [Matching and rankings](matching-and-rankings.md).
