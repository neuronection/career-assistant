# Cover letters

Cover letters are **coming soon** — the creation entry points are gated in the
UI until the feature ships. This page describes the intended behaviour so you
know what to expect.

## What it will do

A cover letter is built **per posting**, from a deterministic brief:

- The posting's **must-haves**
- Your **fit** for the role
- Your **goal** for the application

## Evidence-cited drafts

AI drafts cite your **evidence per paragraph**, and any claim that is not
backed by your profile is **flagged** rather than left to slip through. Drafts
run through the audited AI pipeline like every other AI action.

## Shared rendering and exports

Letters render through the **same template engine** as your CV, using your
CV's design tokens, so a letter and a CV look like a matched set. They share
the same **versions** and **exports** (PDF, DOCX, Markdown, JSON, ATS text).
