# Growth toolkit

Career Assistant keeps working after you are hired. The growth toolkit turns
the same structured data into a plan for getting better, not just getting in.

Open it at `/growth`.

## Roadmaps

Turn skill gaps into tracked steps. Each step has a clear lifecycle:

**done → level self-report → catalog re-fit, visibly**

Marking a step done lets you self-report the new level, and the catalog
re-fits so the change is reflected in your matches — visibly, not silently.

## Near-miss radar

Shows **adjacent roles you are a couple of skills away from**. These are the
highest-leverage moves: close enough to reach, different enough to be worth
considering.

## Learning resources

Close the gaps the roadmap and radar identify, with resources attached to the
specific skills you are missing.

## Market snapshots

Aggregate live postings per role to see what the market is actually asking
for. Thin samples are handled honestly rather than presented as trends.

## Check-ins

- **Quarterly check-ins** keep the plan current.
- **Per-rule quiet hours** keep the nudges discreet (see
  [Notifications and scheduler](notifications-and-scheduler.md)).
