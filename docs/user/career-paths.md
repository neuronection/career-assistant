# Career paths

Every route to a target job in one place: curated paths from the catalog,
your own path copies, and the computed "roads leading here" graph.

Open **Career paths** (`/paths`) from the sidebar. The beta chip is
expected — the surface is new and still growing.

## What you see

- **Path index** (left) — curated paths first, then **My paths** (your own
  copies). Each card shows the target job and your mark, if any.
- **Filters** — narrow the index by target job (code or id), job family or
  your career stage.
- **Path view** — every path renders through the same renderer in three
  views: **Graph** (default), **Tree** and **List**. With *reduced motion*
  enabled in your system, the renderer starts in the Tree view. Switch
  views with the segmented control above the path.
- **Step panel** — select any step to see its skill with your level vs the
  level the target job requires, education notes, and links into the
  catalog, learning resources or your education entries.

## Marks are yours

Every path and every step can be marked:

- **Interested** — this possibility is for me.
- **Pinned** — keep this visible.
- **Dismissed** — not for me; dismissed steps leave the path's
  completeness count.

Marks are preferences. They change what *you* see (and the completeness
count on the path header) — they never change how jobs are scored or
ranked for you.

## Header actions

- **Target job link** — open the destination job's detail page.
- **Completeness** — how many of the path's skill requirements your
  profile already covers.
- **Add to growth roadmap** — creates a tracked roadmap for the target
  job (the Growth toolkit's plan, seeded from your skill gaps).

## Computed routes

Enter a target job reference in the filter and the page also shows the
**computed** routes into that job — the relation graph of careers that
lead there, rendered with the same path renderer. Each job node links
into the catalog.

## Chat-created paths

Ask the assistant in chat ("draft a career path to data science") and it
grounded-drafts a path for you. You review the draft right in the chat
and save it to **My paths** — saved drafts stay **draft** until you
activate them here; nothing the assistant drafts is ever activated
automatically.
